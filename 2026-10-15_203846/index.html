Abstract

Determine, for indigo and epindolidione, whether intramolecular transfer of the nitrogen-bound proton to oxygen connects two minima through a first-order saddle.

Homepage/Indigo and epindolidione proton transferOpen remote

Methods

PM6 relaxed scan, TS, IRC and endpoint frequencies; B3LYP density/ESP

A semiempirical PM6 scan of the proton-transfer coordinate seeds the saddle search, the IRC connects the saddle to both tautomers, and B3LYP/6-31+G(d) single points give density and ESP at the stationary points.
