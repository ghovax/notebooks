Meaning

Options

Option

Value

Meaning

PM6D3

PM6 semi-empirical method with Grimme’s D3 dispersion correction.

Opt

Geometry optimization to a stationary point.

ReadAllGIC

Read the generalized internal coordinates from the checkpoint file.

CalcFC

Compute the force constants at the first point with the current method.

MaxCycles

150

At most 150 optimization steps.

MaxStep

10

Maximum step, the initial trust radius, of 10\times 0.01\,a_0 or 10\times 0.01\,\mathrm{rad}.

RecalcFC

10

Compute analytic second derivatives at the first step and then every 10 steps.

SCF

Controls of the self-consistent field procedure.

Tight

Tight SCF convergence, the default.

XQC

Add a quadratically convergent SCF step if the first-order SCF has not converged.

MaxCycle

512

At most 512 SCF cycles.

NoSymm

Keep the input orientation and do not use molecular symmetry.
