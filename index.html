In progress

The offset-plane saddle converged with one imaginary mode at 530.1194i\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}, forming C–C bonds of 2.2675\,\mathrm{\AA} and a stable closed-shell wavefunction; its IRC branches recorded both directions, ending with the fragments separated 76.6\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} below the saddle and with the ring closed 246.0\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} below it, the reverse terminus converged to a boat-shaped cyclohexene with one imaginary mode rather than a minimum, and the reactant optimization stopped at its 100-step limit with the fragments drifting apart. The coplanar-guess workflow is rejected: it relaxed to a closed cyclohexene-like ring instead of the concerted saddle. A TS outcome map over ethene approach geometries reached the saddle from 6 of its first 15 starts, and the other 9 stopped at the step limit with the fragments drifting apart; the same grid at five further rotations of the diene’s terminal methylene groups is prepared but not submitted.
