概要

We repeat the carbon exchange of indigo and epindolidione without any planar constraint: a PM6-D3 saddle search starts from the maximum of the planar scan, displaced out of plane along the planar saddle’s imaginary modes, with every coordinate free, and a converged first-order saddle is followed along its reaction path along \pm s, and B3LYP fields are prepared at the saddle and both ends.

キーワード
