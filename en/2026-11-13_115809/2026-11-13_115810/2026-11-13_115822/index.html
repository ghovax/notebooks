Goal

  • Calculate the vertical excitations and the relaxed S_1 density’s charges at every converged point of the map, so the S_1 surface and its charge transfer are drawn over the same two twists.

Input

Gaussian input

Link 0 commands

Link 0 sets memory, processors and files.

Route keywords and options

The route selects the method, basis and job type.

Molecule specification

The job’s title, charge and spin multiplicity, and where Gaussian took its geometry from.
