Goal

  • Calculate the vertical S_0\rightarrow S_n energies and oscillator strengths, the relaxed S_1 density’s charges and the S_0\rightarrow S_1 NTOs where \Delta\theta_{\mathrm{B}_1}=160^\circ from the S_0 minimum.

Input

Linked jobs

The input runs 2 jobs in turn, each with its own Link 0 commands, route and section below. The --Link1-- line between two jobs starts the next one at Gaussian’s first link; it numbers no job.

  • Jobs reading the geometry, guess or density an earlier job of the input left in its checkpoint: 2.

Excited states

Link 0 commands

Link 0 is the part of each job before its route: it sets this job’s memory, processors and files. Every linked job has its own.

Route keywords and options

The route selects the method, basis and job type.

Molecule specification

The job’s title, charge and spin multiplicity, and where Gaussian took its geometry from.
