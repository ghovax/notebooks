Goal

  • Map the relaxed S_0 energy over both twists, E(\theta_\mathrm{A},\theta_{\mathrm{B}_1}), on a 30-degree grid, the two-dimensional torsional surface whose cuts the scans follow.

Input

Gaussian input

Link 0 commands

Link 0 sets memory, processors and files.

Route keywords and options

The route selects the method, basis and job type.

Redundant internal coordinate changes

  • ModRedundant adds, freezes, scans or removes redundant internal coordinates before the optimization starts.
  • Gaussian may print a coordinate with its atoms reversed.

Molecule specification

The job’s title, charge and spin multiplicity, and where Gaussian took its geometry from.
