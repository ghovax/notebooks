States

The job solved for 10 excited states. Each state at its wavelength \lambda_n with oscillator strength f_n, over ultraviolet bands.

Oscillator strength measures the transition dipole moment \boldsymbol\mu_{0n} between the ground state and state n, the matrix element of the dipole operator \hat{\boldsymbol\mu}=-\sum_i\hat{\mathbf r}_i (atomic units):

f_n=\frac{2}{3}\,\Delta E_n\,\lvert\boldsymbol\mu_{0n}\rvert^2,\qquad \boldsymbol\mu_{0n}=\langle\Psi_0|\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}|\Psi_n\rangle=-\int\mathbf r\,\rho_{0n}(\mathbf r)\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf r

Without the symmetry of the states, only f_n tells bright states from dark ones.

Each bar stacks one state’s transition weights w=\sum 2c_{ia}^2, c_{ia}^2 per spin when unrestricted; amplitudes below 0.1 are not printed, and dark states, f<0.001, are faded. - The molecule has no symmetry, point group C_1, so orbitals are named from the frontier: \mathrm{HOMO}_{n} below the highest occupied and \mathrm{LUMO}_{n} above the lowest unoccupied.
