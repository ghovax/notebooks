Abstract

Status Workflow Description
Dimethylamino chelate in acetonitrile Tests whether the donor of \mathbf{3} turns perpendicular in S_1, and how much charge moves as it turns.

We study two of the difluoroboron chelates of Saito et al. [1] to test for twisted intramolecular charge transfer (TICT) in acetonitrile, the solvent used here (Fig. 2):

  • The methoxy chelate \mathbf{1} emits strongly in acetonitrile, while the dimethylamino chelate \mathbf{3}, with the stronger donor, barely does; comparing the two separates the donor’s effect from what any such dye does.
  • In their calculation, the S_1 minimum of \mathbf{3} has its donor phenyl nearly perpendicular to the chelate ring, while that of \mathbf{1} stays close to its S_0 structure.

The calculations follow Naito et al. [2], with B3LYP and the dielectric-dependent functional of Shimazaki and Tachikawa [3] in IEFPCM acetonitrile with 6\text{-}31{+}\mathrm{G}(d); since these chelates can twist, the twists are also scanned.

Absorption and emission at the S_0 and S_1 minima

  • S_0 is optimized to \mathbf{R}_0 and verified; there the screening \varepsilon_s and the absorption \Delta E_{S_0\rightarrow S_1} are computed.
  • S_1 is relaxed to \mathbf{R}_1 and verified; there the emission \Delta E_{S_1\rightarrow S_0} is computed.
  • DD-DFT repeats the path with \alpha=1/\varepsilon_s from a self-consistent screening (Fig. 3).

Relaxed scans of the twists about the bonds \mathrm{A} and \mathrm{B}_1

  • From the B3LYP minima, the twist about \mathrm{A} or \mathrm{B}_1 is scanned over half a turn in 10^\circ steps, in S_0 and in S_1, with \Delta E_{S_0\rightarrow S_1} at each S_0 point (Fig. 5).
  • An S_0 map relaxes both twists together on a 30^\circ grid.

Each twist turns one group against the next across its bond:

  • \theta_\mathrm{A} turns the \mathrm{CH_3O} group of \mathbf{1}, or the \mathrm{(CH_3)_2N} group of \mathbf{3}, against the \mathrm{C_6H_4} ring it is bonded to.
  • \theta_{\mathrm{B}_1} turns that \mathrm{C_6H_4} ring against the \mathrm{BF_2O_2C_3H} chelate ring.

At 0^\circ or 180^\circ the two groups lie in one plane and their \pi systems overlap; at 90^\circ they stand at right angles, the overlap is gone, and the excited electron can move wholly from the donor to the acceptor. Fig. 4 shows both angles as each group turns. Naito et al. [2] and Sung et al. [4] compare the same angles between S_0 and S_1.

The chelate chosen in the menu turned rigidly about the chosen bond, in 10^\circ steps over half a turn from its S_0 minimum (or its starting geometry until that is optimized), with \theta_\mathrm{A}, \theta_{\mathrm{B}_1} and the lengths of both bonds measured at every frame; nothing relaxes, so it shows what the scans vary, not their result.

The scans then follow the energy along each twist (Fig. 5): the relaxed S_0 and S_1 profiles, the vertical excitation at each S_0 point, and the barriers read from them.

The absorption and emission runs give, at \mathbf{R}_0 and \mathbf{R}_1, the quantities other studies use to recognize charge transfer (Tab. 1).

Reported values and expected results

Saito et al. [1] report at the S_1 minima, with B3LYP/6\text{-}31{+}\mathrm{G}(d) in PCM acetonitrile:

If our runs agree, we expect:

  • \theta_{\mathrm{B}_1} near 90^\circ at \mathbf{R}_1 of \mathbf{3}, and close to its \mathbf{R}_0 value for \mathbf{1}.
  • The relaxed E_1(\theta_{\mathrm{B}_1}) falling towards 90^\circ for \mathbf{3} and rising for \mathbf{1}.
  • f_1 falling and \lvert\Delta\boldsymbol{\mu}\rvert rising along the twist, more steeply for \mathbf{3}.
  • B3LYP possibly favouring the twisted \mathbf{3} too much, which the DD-DFT branch checks.

NoteOngoing
  • With B3LYP, S_1 of \mathbf{1} is the bright HOMO-to-LUMO state, 2.96\,\mathrm{eV} in absorption and 2.65\,\mathrm{eV} in emission, and has a verified planar minimum.
  • In S_0, turning the methoxy group costs at most 20.5 and the donor ring about \mathrm{B}_1 32.9\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}, and the map’s single maximum of 47.0\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} is close to their sum.
  • Vertically, twisting about \mathrm{B}_1 darkens S_1, to f=0.008 at 100^\circ from 1.28 when planar, while turning the methoxy group raises it by 0.18\,\mathrm{eV} and keeps it bright.
  • Relaxed along \theta_{\mathrm{B}_1}, S_1 stays within 4.2\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} of its planar minimum and reaches a dark perpendicular point 1.9\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} lower, within the Fast level’s precision; relaxed along \theta_\mathrm{A}, S_1 rises by 37.4\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} at the perpendicular methoxy group and stays bright.
  • DD-DFT places S_1 0.37\,\mathrm{eV} higher with less charge transfer; its S_1 minimum awaits verification.

NoteOngoing
  • The S_0 minimum of \mathbf{3} is verified, its B3LYP screening is \varepsilon_s=2.90, and its bright S_1 absorbs at 2.53\,\mathrm{eV}, 0.43\,\mathrm{eV} below the methoxy chelate’s, with more charge transfer.
  • In S_0, turning the dimethylamino group costs 36.8\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}, nearly twice the methoxy group’s, as its nitrogen pyramidalizes, and vertically the turn hands the absorption from S_1 to S_2, f_1 falling to 0.03 at 120^\circ from 1.27.
  • Twisting the donor ring about \mathrm{B}_1 costs 45.7\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} in S_0, 1.4 times the methoxy chelate’s, yet vertically it lowers S_1 from 2.52 to 1.93\,\mathrm{eV} and darkens it to f=0.015 at the perpendicular geometry, where the vertical S_1 lies 11.9\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} below the planar one.
  • Over the S_0 map the twists are not additive: turning both perpendicular costs 65.2\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}, 17.5 less than the two apart, and vertically S_1 is dark wherever the ring about \mathrm{B}_1 is perpendicular or the dimethylamino group is 120^\circ into a sweep, on its uninverted branch.
  • S_1 relaxes into a twisted minimum, the donor ring perpendicular to the core about \mathrm{B}_1, where it is dark (f=1.0\times10^{-4}), only 1.38\,\mathrm{eV} above S_0 and carries a 47.6\,\mathrm{D} dipole; the relaxed S_1 scans are running, and the DD-DFT branch was cancelled and waits to be submitted again.
