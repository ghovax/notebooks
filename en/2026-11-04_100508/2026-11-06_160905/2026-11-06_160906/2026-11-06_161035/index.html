Job setup

Electrons and symmetry

The table gives the system’s electrons, charge, spin and point group; the nuclear charges Z_A and the total charge q set the electron counts and multiplicity:

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad M_S=\frac{N_\alpha-N_\beta}{2},\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Route keywords

Route keywords

The route selects the method, the basis set and the job type.

Link 0 commands

The Link 0 commands set this job’s memory, processors and files.

Molecule

Molecule specification

The specification gives the title, the charge and the spin multiplicity, and where the geometry comes from.

Starting geometry

The viewer shows the structure the input starts from.

Computed results

Molecular orbitals

Frontier energies and gap

The HOMO and LUMO energies set the gap:

\Delta\epsilon=\epsilon_{\mathrm{LUMO}}-\epsilon_{\mathrm{HOMO}}

Levels near the gap

The chart gives each symmetry and spin its own column.

  • Hover a level to name it; click it to highlight it.
  • Without symmetry, point group C_1, levels are counted from the frontier.

Frontier orbitals in space

WarningSurfaces not drawn

Orbital and density surfaces are not drawn for the points of a scan.

Geometry trajectory

The viewer plays every printed geometry in order, earlier frames fading.

Geometry optimization

The optimization made 100 convergence checks; forces and displacements are the maximum and RMS over the optimized coordinates.

Energy at each check

Each check’s energy is given relative to the last, E_n-E_N, so a steady fall to zero marks a smooth optimization.

Forces against their limits

The largest and RMS forces, F_{\max} and F_{\mathrm{RMS}}, are drawn against the limits Gaussian sets for convergence.

Displacements against their limits
