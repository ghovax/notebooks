Goal

  • Map which approach geometries of ethene around s-cis-1,3-butadiene reach the concerted [4+2] saddle, interpolating between the coplanar and the parallel-plane arrangements at several distances.
  • Test whether those outcomes depend on where the diene’s four terminal hydrogens start, by repeating the same grid at fixed rotations of its methylene groups.

The runs of this scan, with the scripts and scans.yaml that generated them, belong to the TS outcome map workflow, whose README gives their method.

Runs

Every run of the scan, placed by the settings it varies; hovering a cell lists its runs with their pages.

The outcome of each start is one of:

  • Forming saddle: One imaginary mode stretching both forming bonds in phase, matching the verified saddle.
  • Other saddle: One imaginary mode that fails those tests.
  • Higher-order saddle: More than one imaginary mode.
  • Minimum: No imaginary mode.
  • Not converged: The optimization or its harmonic analysis did not finish.
