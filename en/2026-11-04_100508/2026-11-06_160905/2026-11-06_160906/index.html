WarningCaveats
  • 41 of the 90 starts reached the verified saddle; the other 49 stopped at the step limit, which shows where this optimizer stops, not that no path exists.
  • Starts with ethene close and face-on reach it most often: 15 of 18 at \theta=90^\circ against 8 of 36 coplanar ones, and 20 of 30 at r=1.29\,\text{\AA} against 6 of 30 at r=2.55\,\text{\AA}.
  • Turning the methylene groups toward the closed ring’s twist helps: 13 of 15 starts reach the saddle at \tau=45^\circ, against 3 of 15 at \tau=-45^\circ and 4 of 15 at \tau=90^\circ.
  • The unrestricted UB3LYP starts reach it in 3 of 18 cases, the closed-shell B3LYP ones in 38 of 72.

Goal

  • Map which approach geometries of ethene around s-cis-1,3-butadiene reach the concerted [4+2] saddle, interpolating between the coplanar and the parallel-plane arrangements at several distances.
  • Test whether those outcomes depend on where the diene’s four terminal hydrogens start, by repeating the same grid at fixed rotations of its methylene groups.

The TS outcome map workflow gives the method of these runs.

Runs

Every run of the scan, placed by the settings it varies; hovering a cell lists its runs with their pages.

The outcome of each start is one of:

  • Forming saddle, meaning one imaginary mode that stretches both forming bonds in phase and matches the verified saddle.
  • Other saddle, meaning one imaginary mode that fails those tests.
  • Higher-order saddle, meaning more than one imaginary mode.
  • Minimum, meaning no imaginary mode.
  • Not converged, meaning the optimization or its harmonic analysis did not finish.
