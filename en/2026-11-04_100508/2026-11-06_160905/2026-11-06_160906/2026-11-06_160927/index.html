Input

System

Symmetry and electrons of the system.

Electron counts and spin follow from the nuclear charges and the charge q:

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad M_S=\frac{N_\alpha-N_\beta}{2},\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Route

Route keywords

Method, basis and job type.

Link 0 commands

This job’s memory, processors and files.

Molecule

Molecule specification

Title, charge, multiplicity and geometry source.

Starting geometry

The input structure.

Output

SCF

83 SCF solutions, one per geometry or state. Cycles per SCF solution.

Geometry

83 convergence checks against their limits: energies E_n-E_N from the last check, forces as maximum and RMS over the optimized coordinates, displacements in bohr and radians.

Trajectory

Every printed geometry in order; earlier frames fade.

Orbitals

HOMO, LUMO and their gap, which is not an excitation energy:

\Delta\epsilon=\epsilon_{\mathrm{LUMO}}-\epsilon_{\mathrm{HOMO}}

Levels near the gap, one column per symmetry:

  • Hover a level to name it; click it to highlight it.
  • The molecule has no symmetry, point group C_1, so orbitals are named from the frontier: \mathrm{HOMO}_{n} below the highest occupied and \mathrm{LUMO}_{n} above the lowest unoccupied.
WarningSurfaces not drawn

The orbital and density surfaces of this run were deliberately not computed yet.
