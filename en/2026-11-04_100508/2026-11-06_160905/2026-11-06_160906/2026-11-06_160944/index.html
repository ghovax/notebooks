Vibrations

Vibrational frequencies

42 modes, 1 imaginary, with unscaled IR intensities over mid-infrared regions.

Wavenumbers \tilde{\nu}_k=\sqrt{k_k/\mu_k}/2\pi c are imaginary where k_k<0; intensities follow I_k\propto\lvert\partial\boldsymbol{\mu}/\partial Q_k\rvert^2.

In C_{s} only modes that change the dipole absorb in the infrared:

  • Infrared-active symmetries are a' (x,y), a'' (z).
  • Every other mode has zero intensity.
Normal-mode frequencies

Negative wavenumbers are imaginary modes.

Normal-mode animations

Imaginary, six lowest and four most IR-intense modes, scaled to a largest displacement of 0.6\,\text{\AA}.

Orbitals

HOMO, LUMO and their gap, which is not an excitation energy:

\Delta\epsilon=\epsilon_{\mathrm{LUMO}}-\epsilon_{\mathrm{HOMO}}

Levels near the gap, one column per symmetry:

  • Hover a level to name it; click it to highlight it.
  • Labels use C_{s} symmetry, with lowercase for orbitals and capitals for states.
  • Gaussian computed in C_{1}, so the labels it printed belong to that group.
