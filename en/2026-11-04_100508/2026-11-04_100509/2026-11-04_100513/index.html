WarningCaveats
  • The closed six-membered ring formed, with forming C–C bonds of 1.55\,\text{\AA}, so the run shows that the reverse IRC branch ends in the cyclohexene ring that the Diels–Alder step is expected to make.
  • The one imaginary mode at 30.6556i\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1} mainly moves the \ce{C5} and \ce{C6} hydrogens and leaves the ring intact, so it reads as the boat ring-inversion saddle of cyclohexene, not as a failure of the ring closure.
  • The minimum check still fails, so this energy is an upper bound on the half-chair minimum and the reaction energy remains unestablished.
Kind Message
Treating 4 low-frequency degrees of freedom as vibrations may cause a significant error in the thermochemistry.
Gaussian found 1 imaginary mode; 0 were expected.
A complete minimum with no imaginary modes is required.

System

The system the job treated, its symmetry and its electrons; the method and basis are those of the input above.

  • N_\alpha and N_\beta count spin-up and spin-down electrons; together they are the nuclear charges Z_A less the charge q.
  • Their difference fixes the spin projection M_S=(N_\alpha-N_\beta)/2; the usual highest-weight input has S=M_S.
  • Closed shells have doubly occupied orbitals; equal spin counts alone do not establish one.

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Geometry optimization

Gaussian printed 5 convergence checks, in output order, against their limits:

  • Each energy E_n is measured from that of the last check printed, E_n-E_N, which is not assumed converged.
  • The forces are given by their largest value and their root mean square over the N coordinates the optimization uses: F_{\max}=\max_i\lvert F_i\rvert,\qquad F_{\mathrm{RMS}}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}F_i^2}
  • Displacements \Delta q are in bohr for distances and radians for angles.

Vibrational frequencies

The latest analysis has 42 modes, 1 of them imaginary, shown with their IR intensities over approximate mid-infrared regions, unscaled:

  • Each wavenumber follows from the mode’s force constant k_k and reduced mass \mu_k: \tilde{\nu}_k=\frac{1}{2\pi c}\sqrt{\frac{k_k}{\mu_k}}
  • The IR intensity grows with the squared change of the dipole along the mode: I_k\propto\left\lvert\frac{\partial\boldsymbol{\mu}}{\partial Q_k}\right\rvert^2
  • A mode is imaginary when k_k<0, a direction of negative curvature.

Frontier orbitals

The highest occupied and lowest unoccupied levels, and their gap, which is not an optical excitation energy:

\Delta\epsilon=\epsilon_{\mathrm{LUMO}}-\epsilon_{\mathrm{HOMO}}

Levels near the gap, one column per symmetry:

  • Hover a level to name it; click it to highlight it.
  • Labels use C_{s} symmetry, with lowercase for orbitals and capitals for states.
  • Gaussian computed in C_{1}, so the labels it printed belong to that group.
  • Surfaces enclose the phases \psi>+\psi_0 and \psi<-\psi_0, holding 85\,\% of \int\psi^2\,\mathrm{d}V; the overall sign is arbitrary.
  • Parts by angular momentum l or atom A, with Mulliken shares summing to 100\,\%: \psi^{(l)}=\sum_{\mu:\,l_\mu=l}c_\mu\phi_\mu,\qquad w_{A,l}=100\sum_{\mu\in A,\,l_\mu=l}c_\mu(\mathbf{S}\mathbf{c})_\mu
