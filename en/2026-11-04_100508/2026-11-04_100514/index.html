ImportantRejected

The stationary structure is cyclohexene-like and its soft imaginary mode does not describe the requested concerted forming-bond saddle.

Kind Message
The smallest \alpha \Delta\epsilon is 0.0403827.
Explicit consideration of 4 degrees of freedom as vibrations may cause significant error.
NoteOngoing
  • The saddle refinement converged to a first-order saddle with one imaginary mode at 530.1194i\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}, forming C–C bonds of 2.2675\,\mathrm{\AA} and a stable closed-shell wavefunction.
  • The IRC branches ran along \pm s, ending 76.6\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} below the saddle with the fragments separated and 246.0\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} below it with the ring closed.
  • The reverse terminus converged to a boat-shaped cyclohexene with one 30.6556i\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1} mode, not a minimum, and the forward-terminus reactant optimization stopped at its 100-step limit with the fragments drifting apart, so neither endpoint is a verified minimum.
  • Variant of the coplanar-guess workflow.
