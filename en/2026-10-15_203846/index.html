Abstract

Determine, for indigo and epindolidione, whether intramolecular transfer of the nitrogen-bound proton to oxygen connects two minima through a first-order saddle.

Workflows

N–H to O–H proton transfer in both frameworks

A semiempirical PM6 scan of the proton-transfer coordinate seeds the saddle search, the IRC connects the saddle to both tautomers, and B3LYP/6\text{-}31{+}\mathrm{G}(d) single points give density and ESP at the stationary points.
