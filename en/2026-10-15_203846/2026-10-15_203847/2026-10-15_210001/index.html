Input

System

Symmetry and electrons of the system.

Electron counts and spin follow from the nuclear charges and the charge q:

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad M_S=\frac{N_\alpha-N_\beta}{2},\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Route

Route keywords

Method, basis and job type.

Link 0 commands

This job’s memory, processors and files.

Molecule

Molecule specification

Title, charge, multiplicity and geometry source.

Starting geometry

The input structure.

Output

SCF

113 SCF solutions, one per geometry or state. Cycles per SCF solution.

Geometry

Maximum and RMS Cartesian forces at each SCF solution, not optimization checks.

Trajectory

Every printed geometry in order; earlier frames fade.

Path

108 points, energies from the saddle, which the dashed line marks.

Orbitals

HOMO, LUMO and their gap, which is not an excitation energy:

\Delta\epsilon=\epsilon_{\mathrm{LUMO}}-\epsilon_{\mathrm{HOMO}}

Levels near the gap, one column per symmetry:

  • Hover a level to name it; click it to highlight it.
  • Labels use C_{s} symmetry, with lowercase for orbitals and capitals for states.
  • Gaussian computed in C_{1}, so the labels it printed belong to that group.
  • Surfaces hold 85\,\% of \int\psi^2\,\mathrm{d}V; the overall sign is arbitrary.
  • Parts by angular momentum l or atom A, with Mulliken shares summing to 100\,\%.

Run

Checkpoints

Saved checkpoints, with energies and gaps, in read/write order.

Resources

Program, resources and timing.
