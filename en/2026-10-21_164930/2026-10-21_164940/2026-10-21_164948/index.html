System

The system the job treated, its symmetry and its electrons; the method and basis are those of the input above.

  • N_\alpha and N_\beta count spin-up and spin-down electrons; together they are the nuclear charges Z_A less the charge q.
  • Their difference fixes the spin projection M_S=(N_\alpha-N_\beta)/2; the usual highest-weight input has S=M_S.
  • Closed shells have doubly occupied orbitals; equal spin counts alone do not establish one.

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Output

SCF convergence

The job recorded 14 self-consistent field solutions, one per geometry or state it evaluated. The chart shows the number of cycles each SCF needed, against its index.

Geometry optimization

Gaussian printed 14 convergence checks, in output order, against their limits:

  • Each energy E_n is measured from that of the last check printed, E_n-E_N, which is not assumed converged.
  • The forces are given by their largest value, F_{\max}=\max_i\lvert F_i\rvert, and their root mean square, F_{\mathrm{RMS}}=\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}F_i^2}, over the N coordinates the optimization uses.
  • Displacements \Delta q are in bohr for distances and radians for angles.

Geometries along the job

Printed geometries in order; faded frames show the preceding structures.

Frontier orbitals

The highest occupied and lowest unoccupied levels, and their gap, which is not an optical excitation energy:

\Delta\epsilon=\epsilon_{\mathrm{LUMO}}-\epsilon_{\mathrm{HOMO}}

Levels near the gap, one column per symmetry:

  • Hover a level to name it; click it to highlight it.
  • Labels use C_{1} symmetry, with lowercase for orbitals and capitals for states.

The orbitals in space, chosen in the dropdowns:

  • Red surfaces enclose \psi<-\psi_0, blue ones \psi>+\psi_0, at the level \psi_0 whose surfaces hold 85\,\% of the orbital’s density \int\psi^2\,\mathrm{d}V; the settings show and move it.
  • The part dropdown keeps the orbital’s terms on one atom or of one angular momentum, at the same isovalue.
  • The pie-chart toggle colours the atoms by that part’s share, the eye toggle hides the surfaces.

Orbital composition

Each atom’s share w_A of the frontier orbitals, from the coefficients \mathbf{c} and the overlap \mathbf{S}, l_\mu being the angular momentum of \phi_\mu:

w_{A,l}=\sum_{\mu\in A,\ l_\mu=l}c_\mu(\mathbf{S}\mathbf{c})_\mu,\qquad w_A=\sum_l w_{A,l}

  • The Part dropdown chooses what the cells show, as in the orbital viewer: each atom group’s w_A, its w_{A,l} of one angular momentum, or one atom’s w_{A,l} of each l, the atom chosen by its element and typed number.
  • Rows are atoms with their hydrogens along the bonds, one bonded fragment after another; groups with w_A<1\% in every set are summed.

Atomic charges and electric moments

The traceless quadrupole moment of the same density, in atomic units and debye ångström:

\mathbf{Q}=\begin{pmatrix}17.389 & 10.2195 & 0.69433 \\ 10.2195 & -14.5789 & -0.755075 \\ 0.69433 & -0.755075 & -2.81008\end{pmatrix}\,\mathrm{e}\,a_0^{2}=\begin{pmatrix}23.3887 & 13.7456 & 0.933898 \\ 13.7456 & -19.6091 & -1.0156 \\ 0.933898 & -1.0156 & -3.77965\end{pmatrix}\,\mathrm{D}\,\text{\AA}
