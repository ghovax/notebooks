System

The system the job treated, its symmetry and its electrons; the method and basis are those of the input above.

  • N_\alpha and N_\beta count spin-up and spin-down electrons; together they are the nuclear charges Z_A less the charge q.
  • Their difference fixes the spin projection M_S=(N_\alpha-N_\beta)/2; the usual highest-weight input has S=M_S.
  • Closed shells have doubly occupied orbitals; equal spin counts alone do not establish one.

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Excited states and spectrum

The job solved for 10 excited states. The spectrum places each state at its wavelength \lambda_n with its oscillator strength f_n, over conventional ultraviolet bands.

Oscillator strength measures the transition dipole moment \boldsymbol\mu_{0n} between the ground state and state n, the matrix element of the dipole operator \hat{\boldsymbol\mu}=-\sum_i\hat{\mathbf r}_i (atomic units):

f_n=\frac{2}{3}\,\Delta E_n\,\lvert\boldsymbol\mu_{0n}\rvert^2,\qquad \boldsymbol\mu_{0n}=\langle\Psi_0|\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}|\Psi_n\rangle=-\int\mathbf r\,\rho_{0n}(\mathbf r)\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf r

In D_{6h}, the closed-shell ground state is ^{1}A_{1g}. Dipole components lie z in A_{2u} and (x,y) in E_{1u}; allowed states satisfy:

\Gamma_0\otimes\Gamma_{\hat{\mathbf r}}\otimes\Gamma_n\ni A_{1g}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Gamma_n\in\lbrace A_{2u},\ E_{1u}\rbrace

An excitation from an orbital set to another transforms as the product of their representations; the signs of the configurations decide whether dipoles cancel. Table 9 groups the leading transitions by their product.

  • Each element R of D_{6h} is coloured by \chi_{a\otimes b}(R)=\chi_a(R)\,\chi_b(R).
  • Where \chi(R)=\pm\chi(E), every state the product forms is symmetric or antisymmetric under R.
  • Rotation axes are long dashes, mirror planes short dashes, and i an open circle.
  • Each operation carries the molecule onto itself; a reflection plays as a flip about an axis in its plane, then the reflection left, and a rotation-reflection then rotates.

Each bar stacks the weights of one state’s transitions between orbital sets:

  • A transition’s weight sums over its printed configurations; an unrestricted job counts c_{ia}^2 per spin instead of 2c_{ia}^2: w=\sum 2c_{ia}^2
  • Configurations with \lvert c_{ia}\rvert<0.1 are not printed, so \sum w\le 100\,\%.
  • Dark states, f<0.001, are faded.
  • Labels use D_{6h} symmetry, with lowercase for orbitals and capitals for states.

Density change of each state

Density changes by state, with degenerate states grouped:

  • Blue surfaces enclose \Delta\rho<-\Delta\rho_0, where electrons leave, and red ones \Delta\rho>+\Delta\rho_0, where they arrive, at the isovalue \Delta\rho_0 whose surfaces hold 80\,\% of each state’s \int\lvert\Delta\rho\rvert\,\mathrm{d}V; the settings show and move it.
  • The densities come from the printed configurations, which leave out amplitudes \lvert c_{ia}\rvert<0.1.

Hole and particle densities integrate to one electron and average degenerate states.

\rho_{\mathrm{h}}=\frac{\sum_{ij}(\mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}})_{ij}\,\phi_i\phi_j}{\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}},\qquad \rho_{\mathrm{p}}=\frac{\sum_{ab}(\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{X})_{ab}\,\phi_a\phi_b}{\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{X}},\qquad \Delta\rho=\rho_{\mathrm{p}}-\rho_{\mathrm{h}}

q_{\mathrm{CT}}=\int\rho_{+}\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf{r},\qquad d_{\mathrm{CT}}=\lvert\mathbf{R}_{+}-\mathbf{R}_{-}\rvert,\qquad S_{\mathrm{hp}}=\int\sqrt{\rho_{\mathrm{h}}\rho_{\mathrm{p}}}\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf{r}

q_{\mathrm{CT}}, d_{\mathrm{CT}} and S_{\mathrm{hp}} measure transferred charge, centroid separation and overlap. The point group D_{6h} holds the centroids of these densities at the molecule’s centre, so d_{\mathrm{CT}} vanishes by symmetry where the table marks it; judge charge transfer from q_{\mathrm{CT}} and S_{\mathrm{hp}} instead.

  • Each element R of D_{6h} is coloured by \chi_\Gamma(R) of the level’s representation \Gamma.
  • A or B means \chi(C_n)=\pm1, g or u means \chi(i)=\pm\chi(E), and ' or '' means \chi(\sigma_h)=\pm\chi(E).
  • Rotation axes are long dashes, mirror planes short dashes, and i an open circle.
  • Each operation carries the molecule onto itself; a reflection plays as a flip about an axis in its plane, then the reflection left, and a rotation-reflection then rotates.

Transition density

NTO pairs contribute according to \lambda_k/\sum_j\lambda_j; each density integrates to one electron:

\rho_{\mathrm{h}}=\frac{\sum_k\lambda_k\lvert\phi_{\mathrm{h},k}\rvert^2}{\sum_k\lambda_k},\qquad \rho_{\mathrm{p}}=\frac{\sum_k\lambda_k\lvert\phi_{\mathrm{p},k}\rvert^2}{\sum_k\lambda_k}.

  • The viewer opens on the difference density \Delta\rho=\rho_{\mathrm{p}}-\rho_{\mathrm{h}}; the loss and the gain can be selected instead.
  • Blue surfaces enclose \Delta\rho<-\Delta\rho_0, where electrons leave, and red ones \Delta\rho>+\Delta\rho_0, where they arrive, at the isovalue \Delta\rho_0 whose surfaces hold 80\,\% of each density’s \int\lvert f\rvert\,\mathrm{d}V; the settings show and move it.
  • \Delta\rho is unrelaxed: exact within TDA, approximate for full TDDFT.

Charge-transfer descriptors use the positive and negative parts of \Delta\rho.

Atomic charge changes from \Delta\rho, where red gains electrons and blue loses them.
