Calculated spectrum

The chart places each state at its wavelength \lambda_n with its oscillator strength f_n, over ultraviolet bands.

Oscillator strength measures the transition dipole moment \boldsymbol\mu_{0n} between the ground state and state n, the matrix element of the dipole operator \hat{\boldsymbol\mu}=-\sum_i\hat{\mathbf r}_i (atomic units):

f_n=\frac{2}{3}\,\Delta E_n\,\lvert\boldsymbol\mu_{0n}\rvert^2,\qquad \boldsymbol\mu_{0n}=\langle\Psi_0|\hat{\boldsymbol\mu}|\Psi_n\rangle=-\int\mathbf r\,\rho_{0n}(\mathbf r)\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf r

In D_{6h}, the closed-shell ground state is ^{1}A_{1g}. Dipole components lie z in A_{2u} and (x,y) in E_{1u}; allowed states satisfy:

\Gamma_0\otimes\Gamma_{\hat{\mathbf r}}\otimes\Gamma_n\ni A_{1g}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Gamma_n\in\lbrace A_{2u},\ E_{1u}\rbrace

An excitation from an orbital set to another transforms as the product of their representations; the signs of the configurations decide whether dipoles cancel. Tab. 8 groups the leading transitions by their product. ###### Symmetry of the transitions

Charge transfer

The hole and particle densities hold one electron each, degenerate states averaged, and q_{\mathrm{CT}}, d_{\mathrm{CT}} and S_{\mathrm{hp}} measure transferred charge, centroid distance and overlap:

\rho_{\mathrm{h}}=\frac{\sum_{ij}(\mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}})_{ij}\,\phi_i\phi_j}{\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}},\qquad \rho_{\mathrm{p}}=\frac{\sum_{ab}(\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{X})_{ab}\,\phi_a\phi_b}{\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{X}},\qquad \Delta\rho=\rho_{\mathrm{p}}-\rho_{\mathrm{h}}
