Screening

Screening from the isotropic uncoupled polarizability over occupied i and virtual a orbitals and the volume V inside density 0.001\,a_0^{-3}, not bulk permittivity:

\bar{\alpha}^{(0)}=\frac{1}{3}\operatorname{tr}\boldsymbol{\alpha}^{(0)} =\frac{4}{3}\sum_{i\in\mathcal O}\sum_{a\in\mathcal V} \frac{\left\lVert\langle\phi_i|\mathbf r|\phi_a\rangle\right\rVert^2}{\epsilon_a-\epsilon_i},\qquad \varepsilon_s=1+\frac{4\pi}{V}\,\bar{\alpha}^{(0)}

Self-consistent screening repeats the SCF until the applied and evaluated \varepsilon_s agree:

E_{\mathrm{XC}}=\alpha\,E_{\mathrm{X}}^{\mathrm{HF}}+(1-\alpha)\,E_{\mathrm{X}}^{\mathrm{B88}}+\sqrt{1-\alpha}\,E_{\mathrm{C}}^{\mathrm{LYP}},\qquad \alpha=\frac{1}{\varepsilon_s}
