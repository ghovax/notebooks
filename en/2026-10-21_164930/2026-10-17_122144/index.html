Abstract

Benzene’s electrons shield each other’s charge. We compute this electronic screening, \varepsilon_s, for one fixed geometry and check that it has converged.

The self-consistent screening and its numerical consistency checks passed.

At fixed nuclear geometry, \varepsilon_s=2.1279013914 after 30 electronic iterations; the final density change is \mathord{1.30\times 10^{-10}} and the screening disagreement is \mathord{3.31\times 10^{-9}}. These satisfy the stated numerical tests. Basis, quadrature and density-contour sensitivity remain untested, and this molecular screening is not the permittivity of bulk liquid benzene.

Lotus determines the molecular electronic screening parameter \varepsilon_s self-consistently for fixed-geometry neutral singlet benzene. This molecular response is not the permittivity of bulk liquid benzene.

Model

  • Cartesian 6\text{-}31\mathrm{G} basis and the supplied ds_svwn model, screened Slater exchange with dielectric-dependent exact exchange and VWN-RPA correlation.
  • Initial \varepsilon_s=3; the density-contour volume uses \rho=0.001\,a_0^{-3} and the orbital response uses q=0.001\,a_0^{-1}.
  • Density-change stopping threshold 10^{-9}, at most 200 electronic iterations, and screening agreement within 10^{-6}.

Molecular geometry

The carbon and hydrogen coordinates remain fixed throughout the electronic calculation (Figure 1).

Screening convergence

Screening updates precede the next numbered electronic iteration and occur intermittently during SCF. The steps show the applied parameter at each update; the final molecular response is reported below (Figure 2).

\varepsilon_s^{(N)}=2.1279013914

Remaining screening difference

The absolute difference from the final response decreases below the 10^{-6} screening acceptance threshold. This numerical agreement does not test basis or quadrature convergence (Figure 3).

Converged result

Electronic and screening checks passed for this input. Basis, quadrature and density-contour sensitivity remain untested.

The calculation record describes the input and model assumptions; the local vault’s Lotus note explains the model (Table 1).
