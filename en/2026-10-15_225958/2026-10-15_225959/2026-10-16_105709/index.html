WarningCaveats

The planar stationary point has the intended in-plane negative mode, but two additional out-of-plane imaginary modes mean it is not a full-space first-order saddle.

Kind Message
Treating rotation classically may cause a significant error in the thermochemistry.
Treating 27 low-frequency degrees of freedom as vibrations may cause a significant error in the thermochemistry.
Gaussian found 3 imaginary modes; 1 was expected.
The saddle was identified under constraints; this alone does not establish a first-order saddle in the full coordinate space.

Input

System

Symmetry and electrons of the system.

Electron counts and spin follow from the nuclear charges and the charge q:

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad M_S=\frac{N_\alpha-N_\beta}{2},\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Route

Route keywords

Method, basis and job type.

Link 0 commands

This job’s memory, processors and files.

Coordinates

ReadAllGIC builds no coordinates of its own; the optimization uses only these generalized internal coordinates. Rows of one form are written for the n-th line, or as its first and last.

Molecule

Molecule specification

Title, charge, multiplicity and geometry source.

Starting geometry

The input structure.

Output

SCF

8 SCF solutions, one per geometry or state. Cycles per SCF solution.

Geometry

8 convergence checks against their limits: energies E_n-E_N from the last check, forces as maximum and RMS over the optimized coordinates, displacements in bohr and radians.

Trajectory

Every printed geometry in order; earlier frames fade.

Vibrations

Vibrational frequencies

84 modes, 3 imaginary, with unscaled IR intensities over mid-infrared regions.

Wavenumbers \tilde{\nu}_k=\sqrt{k_k/\mu_k}/2\pi c are imaginary where k_k<0; intensities follow I_k\propto\lvert\partial\boldsymbol{\mu}/\partial Q_k\rvert^2.

Normal-mode frequencies

Negative wavenumbers are imaginary modes.

Normal-mode animations

Imaginary, six lowest and four most IR-intense modes, scaled to a largest displacement of 0.6\,\text{\AA}.

Orbitals

HOMO, LUMO and their gap, which is not an excitation energy:

\Delta\epsilon=\epsilon_{\mathrm{LUMO}}-\epsilon_{\mathrm{HOMO}}

Levels near the gap, one column per symmetry:

  • Hover a level to name it; click it to highlight it.
  • Labels use C_{2h} symmetry, with lowercase for orbitals and capitals for states.
  • Surfaces hold 85\,\% of \int\psi^2\,\mathrm{d}V; the overall sign is arbitrary.
  • Parts by angular momentum l or atom A, with Mulliken shares summing to 100\,\%.

Properties

Charges and moments

Mulliken charges of the final geometry, red q_A<0 and blue q_A>0, comparable only within one method and basis:
