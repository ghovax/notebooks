Workflows

In-plane carbon exchange

Dispersion-corrected PM6 scans with the molecule held planar locate the in-plane exchange saddle and its IRC, and B3LYP/6\text{-}31{+}\mathrm{G}(d) single points give density and ESP along the path.

Status Period Workflow Description
15–16, 20 Oct 2026 Plane-constrained carbon exchange Follows the in-plane exchange from both molecules through the planar saddle, with the ESP along the path.

Unconstrained carbon exchange

A PM6-D3 saddle search with every coordinate free starts from the maximum of the planar scan, and an IRC follows a converged first-order saddle.

Status Period Workflow Description
10 Nov 2026 Unconstrained carbon exchange Starts from the planar scan maximum, displaced out of plane along the planar saddle’s imaginary modes, with no frozen coordinate and searches the full coordinate space for a first-order saddle and its reaction path, and prepares B3LYP single points at the saddle and both path ends.

Out-of-plane saddle searches

Twisted or out-of-plane starting geometries are refined with PM6-D3 and every coordinate free, and frequencies test whether each converged geometry is a first-order saddle.

Status Period Workflow Description
17 Oct 2026 Out-of-plane central-bond torsion Scans the central-bond dihedral for a twisted starting geometry and tests whether it refines to a stationary torsional saddle.
20 Oct 2026 Indigo displacement TS experiments Refines two out-of-plane displaced geometries of indigo; neither converges to a first-order saddle.
WarningCaveats

The planar saddle has additional out-of-plane negative curvature, so it is not a full-space first-order TS.
