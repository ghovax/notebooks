Drop Gaussian files anywhere on this page to see them as notebooks. They are read in this browser tab, and leave it only when you share them.

Accepted Read as
.log, .out Gaussian or Lotus output, one notebook each
.gjf, .com, .xyz Input, with the output of the same name, or on its own
.fchk, .fch Formatted checkpoint, with the output whose %Chk names it, or on its own
.sdf, .mol Structures, each record in a molecular viewer
