Abstract

Transition-state refinement and IRC following for the parent Diels–Alder reaction of 1,3-butadiene and ethene to cyclohexene.

Keywords

Diels–Alder, transition state, IRC, cyclohexene

Homepage/Diels–Alder reaction of butadiene and ethene/Butadiene and ethene Diels–Alder path from offset planesCalculations Open remote
In progress

The saddle refinement converged to a first-order saddle with one imaginary mode at 530.1194i\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}, forming C–C bonds of 2.2675\,\mathrm{\AA} and a stable closed-shell wavefunction; the IRC branches recorded both directions, with the reverse path stopping on a failed correction step, the reverse terminus converged to a boat-shaped cyclohexene with one imaginary mode rather than a minimum, and the reactant optimization stopped at its 100-step limit with the fragments drifting apart.
