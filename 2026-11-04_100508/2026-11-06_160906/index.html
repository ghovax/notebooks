Abstract

Which approach geometries of ethene around 1,3-butadiene, and which starting rotations of its terminal methylene groups, reach the concerted Diels–Alder saddle under one TS optimization.

Keywords

Diels–Alder, transition state, approach geometry, cyclohexene

Homepage/Diels–Alder reaction of butadiene and ethene/Butadiene and ethene Diels–Alder TS outcome mapCalculations Open remote
In progress

The 15 starts at \tau=0^\circ have finished: six reached the verified saddle, within 0.1\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}} of its energy, and the other nine, including every coplanar start, stopped at the optimizer’s step limit with the fragments drifting apart. The map now also varies the rotation \tau of the diene’s terminal methylene groups, and the 75 starts at the other five rotations are prepared but not submitted.

Optimization paths

Each start, chosen by its method, r, \theta and \tau, shows its optimization path from its initial geometry to its final one, the transition state where it converged, and the frame menu jumps to either end; a start not yet submitted shows its initial geometry alone. Marked points give r and \theta at every frame.

The outcome of each start is one of:

  • Forming saddle: One imaginary mode stretching both forming bonds in phase, matching the verified saddle.
  • Other saddle: One imaginary mode that fails those tests.
  • Higher-order saddle: More than one imaginary mode.
  • Minimum: No imaginary mode.
  • Not converged: The optimization or its harmonic analysis did not finish.
