System

The system the job treated, its symmetry and its electrons; the method and basis are those of the input above.

  • N_\alpha and N_\beta count spin-up and spin-down electrons; together they are the nuclear charges Z_A less the charge q.
  • Their difference fixes the spin projection M_S=(N_\alpha-N_\beta)/2; the usual highest-weight input has S=M_S.
  • Closed shells have doubly occupied orbitals; equal spin counts alone do not establish one.

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Vibrational frequencies

The latest analysis has 42 modes, 1 of them imaginary, shown with their IR intensities over approximate mid-infrared regions, unscaled:

  • Each wavenumber follows from the mode’s force constant and reduced mass, \tilde{\nu}_k=\frac{1}{2\pi c}\sqrt{k_k/\mu_k}.
  • The IR intensity grows with the squared change of the dipole along the mode, I_k\propto\lvert\partial\boldsymbol{\mu}/\partial Q_k\rvert^2.
  • A mode is imaginary when k_k<0, a direction of negative curvature.

In C_{s} only modes that change the dipole absorb in the infrared:

  • Infrared-active symmetries are a' (x,y), a'' (z).
  • Every other mode has zero intensity.

Orbital composition

Each atom’s share w_A of the frontier orbitals, from the coefficients \mathbf{c} and the overlap \mathbf{S}, l_\mu being the angular momentum of \phi_\mu:

w_{A,l}=\sum_{\mu\in A,\ l_\mu=l}c_\mu(\mathbf{S}\mathbf{c})_\mu,\qquad w_A=\sum_l w_{A,l}

  • One matrix per fragment, its rows atoms with their hydrogens along the bonds; groups with w_A<1\% in every set are summed.
  • Hover a cell for its parts w_{A,l}.
