System

The system the job treated, its symmetry and its electrons; the method and basis are those of the input above.

  • N_\alpha and N_\beta count spin-up and spin-down electrons; together they are the nuclear charges Z_A less the charge q.
  • Their difference fixes the spin projection M_S=(N_\alpha-N_\beta)/2; the usual highest-weight input has S=M_S.
  • Closed shells have doubly occupied orbitals; equal spin counts alone do not establish one.

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Oscillator strength measures the transition dipole (atomic units):

f_n=\frac{2}{3}\,\Delta E_n\left|\langle\Psi_0|\hat{\mathbf r}|\Psi_n\rangle\right|^2,\qquad \langle\Psi_0|\hat{\mathbf r}|\Psi_n\rangle=\int\mathbf r\,\rho_{0n}(\mathbf r)\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf r

In D_{6h}, the closed-shell ground state is ^{1}A_{1g}. Dipole components lie z in A_{2u} and (x,y) in E_{1u}; allowed states satisfy:

\Gamma_0\otimes\Gamma_{\hat{\mathbf r}}\otimes\Gamma_n\ni A_{1g}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Gamma_n\in\lbrace A_{2u},\ E_{1u}\rbrace

Excitations transform as orbital-representation products; configuration signs determine dipole cancellation.

\begin{aligned} \Gamma(1e_{1g}\rightarrow1e_{2u})&=e_{1g}\otimes e_{2u}=B_{1u}\oplus B_{2u}\oplus E_{1u} \\ \Gamma(3e_{2g}\rightarrow1e_{2u})&=e_{2g}\otimes e_{2u}=A_{1u}\oplus A_{2u}\oplus E_{2u} \\ \Gamma(1e_{1g}\rightarrow4a_{1g})&=e_{1g}\otimes a_{1g}=E_{1g} \end{aligned}

Hole and particle densities integrate to one electron and average degenerate states.

\rho_{\mathrm{h}}=\frac{\sum_{ij}(\mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}})_{ij}\,\phi_i\phi_j}{\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}},\qquad \rho_{\mathrm{p}}=\frac{\sum_{ab}(\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{X})_{ab}\,\phi_a\phi_b}{\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{X}},\qquad \Delta\rho=\rho_{\mathrm{p}}-\rho_{\mathrm{h}}

q_{\mathrm{CT}}=\int\rho_{+}\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf{r},\qquad d_{\mathrm{CT}}=\lvert\mathbf{R}_{+}-\mathbf{R}_{-}\rvert,\qquad S_{\mathrm{hp}}=\int\sqrt{\rho_{\mathrm{h}}\rho_{\mathrm{p}}}\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf{r}

q_{\mathrm{CT}}, d_{\mathrm{CT}} and S_{\mathrm{hp}} measure transferred charge, centroid separation and overlap. Inversion symmetry makes d_{\mathrm{CT}} vanish; use q_{\mathrm{CT}} and S_{\mathrm{hp}} instead.
