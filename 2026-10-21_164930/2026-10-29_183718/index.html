Abstract

B3LYP and DD-DFT molecular screening and vertical absorption of benzene, with a fresh electronic guess for each Gaussian calculation.

Keywords

benzene, independent guesses, molecular screening, vertical absorption

Homepage/Molecular screening \varepsilon_s and S_0\rightarrow S_n excitations/Benzene with independent guessesCalculations Open remote
Accepted

Without checkpoint reuse, the B3LYP and DD-DFT spectra match the checkpoint-chained ones within 3\,\mathrm{meV}, so checkpoint reuse did not change the benzene results. A second geometry/screening cycle makes the DD-DFT weights self-consistent with the geometry and moves the spectrum by at most 2.7\,\mathrm{meV}.

This workflow supersedes the checkpoint-chained comparison, using independently generated electronic guesses.
