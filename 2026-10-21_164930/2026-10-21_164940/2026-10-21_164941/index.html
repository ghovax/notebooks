Route keywords and options

The route selects the method, basis set and job type. Each keyword is listed with its meaning, followed by its options, as Gaussian read them.

Keyword

Meaning

Options

Option

Value

Meaning

B3LYP

B3LYP hybrid density functional: Becke three-parameter exchange with LYP correlation.

6-31G(d,p)

Pople split-valence double-zeta basis with d polarization functions on heavy atoms and p functions on hydrogen; also written 6-31G**.

NoSymm

Keep the input orientation and do not use molecular symmetry.

Int

Computation of the two-electron integrals and the DFT integration grid.

UltraFine

Pruned (99,590) integration grid: 99 radial shells with 590 angular points each.

SCF

Controls of the self-consistent field procedure.

Tight

Tight SCF convergence, the default.

XQC

Add a quadratically convergent SCF step if the first-order SCF has not converged.

MaxCycle

512

At most 512 SCF cycles.

Opt

Geometry optimization to a stationary point.

CalcFC

Compute the force constants at the first point with the current method.

MaxStep

10

MaxCycles

200

SCRF
