The system the job treated, its symmetry and its electrons; the method and basis are those of the input above.

  • N_\alpha and N_\beta count spin-up and spin-down electrons; together they are the nuclear charges Z_A less the charge q.
  • Their difference fixes the spin projection M_S=(N_\alpha-N_\beta)/2; the usual highest-weight input has S=M_S.

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Orbital composition

w_{A,l}=\sum_{\mu\in A,\ l_\mu=l}c_\mu(\mathbf{S}\mathbf{c})_\mu,\qquad w_A=\sum_l w_{A,l}

  • One matrix per fragment, its rows atoms with their hydrogens along the bonds; groups with w_A<1\% in every set are summed.

In C_{1}, the closed-shell ground state is ^{1}A. Dipole components lie (x,y,z) in A; allowed states satisfy:

\Gamma_0\otimes\Gamma_{\hat{\mathbf r}}\otimes\Gamma_n\ni A\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Gamma_n\in\lbrace A\rbrace

Hole and particle densities integrate to one electron and average degenerate states.

\rho_{\mathrm{h}}=\frac{\sum_{ij}(\mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}})_{ij}\,\phi_i\phi_j}{\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}},\qquad \rho_{\mathrm{p}}=\frac{\sum_{ab}(\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{X})_{ab}\,\phi_a\phi_b}{\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{X}},\qquad \Delta\rho=\rho_{\mathrm{p}}-\rho_{\mathrm{h}}

q_{\mathrm{CT}}=\int\rho_{+}\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf{r},\qquad d_{\mathrm{CT}}=\lvert\mathbf{R}_{+}-\mathbf{R}_{-}\rvert,\qquad S_{\mathrm{hp}}=\int\sqrt{\rho_{\mathrm{h}}\rho_{\mathrm{p}}}\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf{r}

q_{\mathrm{CT}}, d_{\mathrm{CT}} and S_{\mathrm{hp}} measure transferred charge, centroid separation and overlap.

Transition density

NTO pairs contribute according to \lambda_k/\sum_j\lambda_j; each density integrates to one electron:

\rho_{\mathrm{h}}=\frac{\sum_k\lambda_k\lvert\phi_{\mathrm{h},k}\rvert^2}{\sum_k\lambda_k},\qquad \rho_{\mathrm{p}}=\frac{\sum_k\lambda_k\lvert\phi_{\mathrm{p},k}\rvert^2}{\sum_k\lambda_k}.

  • Select loss, gain or difference density \Delta\rho=\rho_{\mathrm{p}}-\rho_{\mathrm{h}}.
  • Blue surfaces enclose \Delta\rho<-0.001\,a_0^{-3}, where electrons leave, and red ones \Delta\rho>+0.001\,a_0^{-3}, where they arrive.
  • \Delta\rho is unrelaxed: exact within TDA, approximate for full TDDFT.
