System

The system the job treated, its symmetry and its electrons; the method and basis are those of the input above.

  • N_\alpha and N_\beta count spin-up and spin-down electrons; together they are the nuclear charges Z_A less the charge q.
  • Their difference fixes the spin projection M_S=(N_\alpha-N_\beta)/2; the usual highest-weight input has S=M_S.
  • Closed shells have doubly occupied orbitals; equal spin counts alone do not establish one.

N_\alpha+N_\beta=\sum_A Z_A-q,\qquad 2S+1=N_\alpha-N_\beta+1

Geometry optimization

Gaussian printed 20 convergence checks, in output order, against their limits:

  • Each energy E_n is measured from that of the last check printed, E_n-E_N, which is not assumed converged.
  • The forces are given by their largest value, F_{\max}=\max_i\lvert F_i\rvert, and their root mean square, F_{\mathrm{RMS}}=\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}F_i^2}, over the N coordinates the optimization uses.
  • Displacements \Delta q are in bohr for distances and radians for angles.

Orbital composition

Each atom’s share w_A of the frontier orbitals, from the coefficients \mathbf{c} and the overlap \mathbf{S}, l_\mu being the angular momentum of \phi_\mu:

w_{A,l}=\sum_{\mu\in A,\ l_\mu=l}c_\mu(\mathbf{S}\mathbf{c})_\mu,\qquad w_A=\sum_l w_{A,l}

  • One matrix per fragment, its rows atoms with their hydrogens along the bonds; groups with w_A<1\% in every set are summed.
  • Hover a cell for its parts w_{A,l}.

Oscillator strength measures the transition dipole (atomic units):

f_n=\frac{2}{3}\,\Delta E_n\left|\langle\Psi_0|\hat{\mathbf r}|\Psi_n\rangle\right|^2,\qquad \langle\Psi_0|\hat{\mathbf r}|\Psi_n\rangle=\int\mathbf r\,\rho_{0n}(\mathbf r)\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf r

In C_{1}, the closed-shell ground state is ^{1}A. Dipole components lie (x,y,z) in A; allowed states satisfy:

\Gamma_0\otimes\Gamma_{\hat{\mathbf r}}\otimes\Gamma_n\ni A\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Gamma_n\in\lbrace A\rbrace

Excitations transform as orbital-representation products; configuration signs determine dipole cancellation.

\begin{aligned} \Gamma(89a\rightarrow90a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(88a\rightarrow90a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(87a\rightarrow90a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(86a\rightarrow90a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(85a\rightarrow90a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(89a\rightarrow91a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(83a\rightarrow90a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(84a\rightarrow90a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(89a\rightarrow93a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(87a\rightarrow91a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(88a\rightarrow92a)&=a\otimes a=A \\ \Gamma(89a\rightarrow92a)&=a\otimes a=A \end{aligned}

Density change of each state

Density changes by state, with degenerate states grouped:

  • Blue surfaces enclose \Delta\rho<-0.001\,a_0^{-3}, where electrons leave, and red ones \Delta\rho>+0.001\,a_0^{-3}, where they arrive.
  • The densities come from the printed configurations, which leave out amplitudes \lvert c_{ia}\rvert<0.1.

Hole and particle densities integrate to one electron and average degenerate states.

\rho_{\mathrm{h}}=\frac{\sum_{ij}(\mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}})_{ij}\,\phi_i\phi_j}{\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{X}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}},\qquad \rho_{\mathrm{p}}=\frac{\sum_{ab}(\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{X})_{ab}\,\phi_a\phi_b}{\operatorname{tr}\mathbf{X}^{\mathsf{T}}\mathbf{X}},\qquad \Delta\rho=\rho_{\mathrm{p}}-\rho_{\mathrm{h}}

q_{\mathrm{CT}}=\int\rho_{+}\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf{r},\qquad d_{\mathrm{CT}}=\lvert\mathbf{R}_{+}-\mathbf{R}_{-}\rvert,\qquad S_{\mathrm{hp}}=\int\sqrt{\rho_{\mathrm{h}}\rho_{\mathrm{p}}}\,\mathrm{d}\mathbf{r}

q_{\mathrm{CT}}, d_{\mathrm{CT}} and S_{\mathrm{hp}} measure transferred charge, centroid separation and overlap.
